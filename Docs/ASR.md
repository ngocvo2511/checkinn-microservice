# Architectural Significant Requirements (ASR) Mapping - Final Revision

| ID | Module | Function | Description | Architecture Requirement Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Identity & Access Management** | Login, Register, Forgot Password | Allows users to create accounts, authenticate, and recover passwords via email. | **Security:**<br>- *Password Policy & Storage:* Enforce strong password policies (e.g., min 12 chars) and securely hash credentials to prevent unauthorized access.<br>- *Authentication Integrity:* Generate and validate 6-digit OTPs strictly within a 10-minute window.<br><br>**Performance:**<br>- *Login Latency:* Login should complete within 500ms under normal load.<br><br>**Interoperability:**<br>- *External Service Fault Tolerance:* Handle third-party email service timeouts gracefully. |
| 2 | **Search & Discovery** | Search Hotels | Customers search hotels by destination, dates, guests, and room quantity. | **Performance:**<br>- *Latency / Search Optimization:* Search results should be returned within 2 seconds under normal load. |
| 3 | **Booking & Payment Engine** | Room Hold, Booking, VNPay, Hotel Payment | Customers create bookings, hold room inventory, and complete payment. | **Reliability:**<br>- *Concurrency Control:* Ensure inventory is never over-allocated when multiple users attempt to book the last room simultaneously.<br>- *Distributed Transactions:* When a customer starts booking, the system must create a temporary room hold. If VNPay payment fails, the booking must remain unconfirmed and the hold must be kept until expiration to allow retry. If the hold expires without successful payment, the booking and active payment attempt must be cancelled.<br>- *Asynchronous Task Execution:* When a room hold expires without successful payment, the booking service must periodically cancel expired PENDING or PENDING_PAYMENT bookings and their active payment attempts, while the hotel service must periodically release the expired room hold from room availability without manual intervention.<br><br>**Interoperability:**<br>- *VNPay Integration:* When VNPay is unavailable or returns an error, the system must handle the failure gracefully and prevent the booking from being incorrectly confirmed.<br><br>**Modifiability:**<br>- *Payment Method Extension:* When a new payment method is added, the payment flow should be extendable without changing the core booking creation logic. |
| 4 | **Hotel Owner Portal** | Hotel Owner Management | Hotel owners manage their specific properties, rooms, incoming bookings, and customer reviews. | **Security:**<br>- *Role-Based Authorization (RBAC):* Strictly enforce data isolation so a Hotel Owner can only view and manage their own registered properties. |
| 5 | **Analytics & Reporting** | System Reports | Admin and Hotel Owners generate revenue, occupancy, and platform overview reports. | **Performance:**<br>- *Throughput / Batch Processing:* Aggregate large volumes of transactional data, including booking records, payment records, revenue records, and reviews, without degrading the response time of active customer sessions. |
| 6 | **Content Management** | Reviews, Help Center, Policy Documents, Media Storage | Customers submit reviews for completed stays, and hotel owners view and respond to reviews for their hotels; Admin manages Help Center policy documents and support content. | **Security:**<br>- *Input Sanitization:* Validate customer review content before storing it; if profanity filtering is required, inappropriate terminology must be rejected.<br>- *File Upload Integrity:* Strictly validate uploaded file formats (.doc, .docx, .pdf) to prevent malicious scripts.<br><br>**Scalability:**<br>- *Storage Management:* Efficiently manage and retrieve approximately 500,000 to 1,000,000 business records and 50,000 to 100,000 media/support files, with an estimated data growth rate of 50MB to 200MB per day from hotel media, booking records, payment records, reviews, reports, and Help Center or policy documents. |
| 7 | **System Administration** | Configuration & Governance | Admin updates system regulations, deletes categories, and bans users. | **Modifiability:**<br>- *Dynamic Configurability:* Ensure new regulations (fees, pricing limits) take effect immediately across all transactions without a system restart.<br><br>**Auditability:**<br>- *Action Traceability:* Securely log highly sensitive actions (e.g., banning users) and automatically trigger email notifications. |
| 8 | **Infrastructure** | System Operations | Overall system concurrency handling, availability, and scheduled maintenance limits. | **Performance:**<br>- *Concurrency:* Architecture must support and efficiently handle up to 150 concurrent users interacting with the platform during peak operations.<br><br>**Availability:**<br>- *System Uptime & Maintenance Window:* Maintain 95% uptime and restrict major deployments to a strict 1-to-2-hour window on Sunday at 11 PM.<br>- *Fault Tolerance:* Isolate non-critical service failures (e.g., Review Service) using Circuit Breakers to ensure core booking/search functionalities remain uninterrupted for users.<br>- *System Recoverability:* Implement automated health checks and failover mechanisms (via Load Balancers and Replicas) to seamlessly redirect users if a server node crashes during operation. |
